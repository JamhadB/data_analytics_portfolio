# Data Analytics Portfolio

> These are projects I have worked / am working on to improve my data skills. Some are redressed projects from the past that I have recently uploaded to Github. I have a BS in Physics from the University of Texas at Austin and am a Marine Corps veteran currently transitioning into data-focused roles. My projects reflect a statistics-first approach: hypothesis testing, careful exploratory analysis, and honest reporting of model limitations, rather than just running an algorithm and reporting a number.

This is a collection of data analytics projects I completed for various academic, self-learning, and applied purposes. They are presented as R markdown documents, python notebooks, and pdfs.

*Data used in these projects is for demonstration and educational purposes only.*

## Contents

### Statistical Analysis (R)

- **[Stellar Classification and Redshift: A Deeper Analysis of SDSS Data](https://github.com/JamhadB/data_analytics_portfolio/tree/main/stellar_classification_analysis)**: Investigating whether right ascension and object classification are associated with redshift in Sloan Digital Sky Survey data. Uses chi-squared tests, two-sample t-tests, and multivariate regression. Including identifying and correcting for a confounding variable that a simpler model missed.

- **[Barton Springs Salamander Analysis](https://github.com/JamhadB/data_analytics_portfolio/tree/main/barton_springs_salamanders)**: Investigating whether dissolved oxygen (DO) differences across four Barton Springs sites can be explained by survey effort or salamander detection rates, rather than the sites themselves. Includes a detailed data cleaning process that uncovered and corrected a unit-mixing error (percent saturation vs. mg/L) in the raw data, a baseline regression showing DO varies significantly by site even after accounting for time trends, and an honest discussion of why a formal confounding test wasn't feasible given the data's large gaps. This was instead resolved through a triangulated, descriptive argument. Collaborative project with Nick Gonzalez.

Tools: R, tidyverse, ggplot2
